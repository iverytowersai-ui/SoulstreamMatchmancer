# **Soulstream: Character Wardrobe & Art Direction Guide**

This document outlines the visual evolution and "Transformation Beats" for the primary characters of the Soulstream saga. It is designed to assist in character design, sprite creation, and AI-assisted art generation for the *Matchmancer* project.

## **🎨 Global Visual Aesthetics**

* **The Mancer Glow:** Magical transformations should be accompanied by a "shimmering" or "glitch" effect (Skin tingling, hair changing color).  
* **Fabric Alchemy:** Materials should look unconventional—liquid metal, embroidered shadows, and deconstructed antique silks.  
* **The Mirror Contrast:** Characters often have a "Mundane" version and a "Mancer" version.

## **🎭 Character Evolution & Transformation Paths**

### **1\. Estar / Star ("The Glamourist")**

**The Core Arc:** From "Hidden" to "Hyper-Visible."

* **Look A (The Mancer):** Oversized gray hoodie, thick black-rimmed glasses, unstyled brown hair.  
* **Look B (The Spark):** The first mirror shift. Glasses removed, hair becomes vibrant crimson-red with honey-blonde highlights, glossy "glass-skin" makeup.  
* **Look C (The Club Glamour):** Magical haute couture. A backless, sparkly dress that seems to catch every light source; fabrics that look like liquid stars.  
* **Look D (The Olympus Heir):** High-fashion "Little Red Dress," 6-inch stilettos, expensive jewelry, and a crown-like confidence.

### **2\. Sky ("The Architect")**

**The Core Arc:** From "Socialite" to "Creator-Goddess."

* **Look A (The Modern It-Girl):** Micro-skirts, designer camisoles, high-fashion street style.  
* **Look B (The Deconstructor):** A shredded Zalomoni antique dress. Raw edges, hand-torn silk, tailored specifically to look "messy-chic."  
* **Look C (The Sculptor):** Often seen with tools or glowing hands, modifying the appearances of others (like the Diva-to-Star shift).

### **3\. Nina ("The Reluctant Goddess")**

**The Core Arc:** From "Dirty Tomboy" to "Golden Legend."

* **Look A (The Outdoorsy):** Self-cut short hair, practical hiking/climbing gear, dirt-smudged cheeks.  
* **Look B (The Wreath-Born):** A wreath of complex golden braids (Star’s work), shimmering skin, ethereal makeup.  
* **Look C (The Past Life):** Golden-braided warrior-monk. Light leather/metal armor, flowing robes, and a long, singular braid.

### **4\. Blue ("The Kinetic")**

**The Core Arc:** From "Casual" to "Reinforced Warrior."

* **Look A (The Street Kid):** Basic hoodies and jeans, always wearing his signature combat gloves.  
* **Look B (The Battle Spec):** Sky-crafted reinforced motorcycle jacket. Heavy padding, tactical aesthetics, tailored for movement.  
* **Look C (The Reset):** Post-explosion/naked state. Represents his most vulnerable, stripped-back self.

## **📸 Image Generation Prompts (Matchmancer Assets)**

Use these prompts in your preferred AI art tool to generate consistent character concepts.

### **Star (Transformation Scene)**

**Prompt:** A high-fashion character concept of a woman named Star. Half the image shows a woman in a baggy gray hoodie and glasses (Estar). The other half shows her transformation: brilliant crimson hair with blonde highlights, glossy red lips, wearing a backless sparkly haute couture gown made of liquid starlight. Cinematic lighting, ethereal magic particles, 8k resolution, artstation style.

### **Sky (The Deconstructed Queen)**

**Prompt:** A powerful female fashion architect named Sky. She is wearing a shredded, deconstructed antique Zalomoni dress made of ivory silk and gold embroidery. The dress has raw, torn edges but looks like high-fashion couture. Sharp cheekbones, piercing eyes, holding a tailor's shears. High-fashion photography style, royal palace background.

### **Nina (The Warrior Monk)**

**Prompt:** A fantasy character concept of Nina. A golden-braided monk wearing light, intricate leather armor over white linen robes. She has a singular long golden braid reaching her waist. Her expression is fierce and focused. Behind her, a faint ghostly image of a dirty tomboy in modern hiking gear. Ornate fantasy background, digital painting, soft volumetric lighting.

### **Blue (The Kinetic Combatant)**

**Prompt:** A young man named Blue in a tactical urban combat outfit. He is wearing a custom-tailored black reinforced motorcycle jacket with silver accents and heavy-duty combat gloves. Kinetic energy ripples around his hands. Gritty urban setting, neon lights, cyberpunk-fantasy fusion, highly detailed textures.

### **Ahlina (The Punk Rebel)**

**Prompt:** A punk-rock character concept. Female with an asymmetric hairstyle: one side shaved and dyed hot pink, the other side long and platinum blonde. Wearing a vintage jean jacket with patches and 80s-style leg warmers. 90s street photography style, rebellious energy, vibrant colors.

## **🛠 Transformation Mechanic Ideas for Matchmancer**

* **Wardrobe Swap:** When a player activates a "Glamour" ability, the character sprite "glitches" and swaps from the Mundane version to the Mancer version for 3 turns.  
* **Mirror Reflection:** The background of the character portrait should show the *other* version of themselves (e.g., Star’s portrait shows Estar in the reflection).  
* **Destruction Level:** For Blue, his clothes can visibly tear or vanish as his "Power Bar" reaches its limit, reflecting the scene where his clothes are destroyed by his own energy.
