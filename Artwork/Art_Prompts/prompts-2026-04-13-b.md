## Refinement Notes (All Prompts)

- **If silhouettes feel weak:** Increase contrast between character and background. Add rim lighting from the character's power source color.
- **If materials feel cheap:** Emphasize matte over glossy. Add wear details — scuffs, patina, creases, tarnish. Remove any accidental shiny surfaces.
- **If energy/glow feels decorative:** Reduce glow radius, increase internal structure. Energy should have direction and channels, not ambient bloom.
- **If the three-register balance is off:** Check that each prompt has dark fantasy weight (materials, mood), whimsical charm (personality, expression), and sci-fi edge (energy behavior, tech details). Adjust the weakest register.
- **For Crux:** If the grin doesn't read, make it wider and add squinted eyes. The personality IS the silhouette for this character.
- **For Kimmy:** If the transformation looks generic, exaggerate the size difference. The human form should be tiny relative to the werewolf mass.
- **For Yanoro:** If the shadow-dash looks like regular smoke, add geometric phase-shift edges to the smoke — it should feel like reality tearing, not fog.
- **For Mr. Violet:** If he looks like a regular squirrel, push the tail glow harder and add the cosmic silhouette more prominently. The contrast between cute and cosmic IS the design.
- **For Chanel Dark Bride:** If the bride aesthetic overwhelms the Vampyl identity, push the blood Magick harder and darken the lighting. She's a predator in a costume, not a bride.
