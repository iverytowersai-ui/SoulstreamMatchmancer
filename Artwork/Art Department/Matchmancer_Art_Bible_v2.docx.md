## **1.2 Key Organizations**

**OZONE:** The most powerful secret society in the world. They assassinate Supernaturals globally, with operations going back centuries. They have satellites, government-grade tech, military snipers. Their mark appears on satellites and equipment. Visual identity: cold, technological, surveillance-state aesthetic. Steel, digital, clinical.

**Petsha Family:** One of the world's oldest and most powerful Gypsy families. Masters of stealth, escape, and hiding. They can see things ordinary people cannot. They carry coins, pebbles, and small charms. Visual identity: warm, earthy, amber-gold, handcrafted metalwork, caravan culture meets supernatural.

**Coven of Unity:** A new coven formed by Witchbreed who broke away from their original covens to unite. Led by Kaery. Members come from the Warlocks, Chaos Kids, Gore-hounds, Dragons, Beasts, Faerykin, and more. Visual identity: diverse, unified by a shared seal/symbol, mix of all coven aesthetics.

# **6\. Visual Style Rules**

These rules ensure visual consistency across all assets. Pin this section to your wall.

**Rule 1 — Magick is organic, technology is cold.** Witchbreed/Mancer Magick always looks living, flowing, warm-edged. OZONE technology always looks clinical, angular, cold-blue. Never mix these aesthetics within a single asset.

**Rule 2 — Every color has a meaning.** Blue-white \= Soulstream energy. Violet-purple \= Witchbreed coven Magick. Crimson-red \= Witchbreed identity/thorns. Amber-gold \= Petsha/probability. Teal-cyan \= portal/dimensional. Steel-blue \= OZONE tech. Gold-white \= divine/Mancer power.

**Rule 3 — Darkness is the canvas.** The Soulstream universe is dark but not depressing. Light comes from supernatural sources (glowing runes, Magick energy, Soulstream rifts, candlelight). Never use flat daylight or studio lighting.

**Rule 4 — Readability over detail.** A beautiful tile that's unreadable at 60x60px is useless. Tiles are ICONS first, art second. Characters are portraits first, paintings second. The phone screen is tiny. Design for it.

**Rule 5 — The reference image is energy, not design.** Your uploaded character sheet (crimson energy, dark fantasy warrior) captures the MOOD and ENERGY quality you want. Apply that energy level to the actual book-accurate characters, don't copy the character design itself.

**Rule 6 — One variable per iteration.** When generating with AI tools: change only lighting OR pose OR material OR color per attempt. Batch changes produce chaos. Systematic refinement produces studio quality.
