# Soulstream: Matchmancer — Character Design Moodboard

**Purpose:** Character Roster Visual Direction  
**Date:** April 13, 2026  
**Based on:** 9 reference images mapped to the full launch roster  

---

## Moodboard Purpose (Step 1)

This moodboard defines the **character art style** for all 9 launch heroes/villains plus familiars in Soulstream: Matchmancer. The visual identity blends three registers simultaneously:

- **Dark Fantasy** — Vampyl generals, shadow magick, ritual weight, contained danger
- **Whimsical** — Cute familiars, pastel accents, playful charm that balances the darkness
- **Sci-Fi Fantasy** — OZONE tech suits, holograms, Technomancy, cyberpunk streetwear

The rendering target is semi-realistic with stylized proportions — inspired by Joe Madureira / Battle Chasers line quality, Solo Leveling manhwa drama, and AFK Arena portrait polish. Premium mobile, not mobile-casual.

## Reference Image Mapping (Steps 2-3)

### Ref 1 — SURGE-OVERLOAD (Goddess of Technology)
**Reference:** Cyberpunk punk girl, pink/blue split pigtails, plaid crop top + skirt, leather jacket with teal-glowing tech panels, energy orb in hand

**What it nails:** The plaid + leather + LED accent combo is exactly Surge-Overload's aesthetic. The nonchalant attitude, dramatic makeup, and tech-integrated clothing hit the right tone. The mismatched hair colors capture her "goth + hip hop + punk" fusion.

**Adjustments needed for final art:**
- Add her signature floating holographic AI screen beside her
- Include the constant lollipop
- Shift power color from teal to matrix green + electric cyan
- Add the Technomancy sigil spinning as her ground rune
- Oversized neo-futuristic shoes

**Power color:** Matrix green + electric cyan  
**School:** Technomancy

---

### Ref 2 — KAERY (Queen of the Witchbreed)
**Reference:** Purple-haired witch, translucent starlit cape, ornate staff with purple flame, witch hat, corset dress, ethereal presence

**What it nails:** The purple power color, the ethereal flowing fabric with embedded starlight, and the "soft but fierce" energy are spot-on. The translucent cape with sparkle/star effects is a strong reference for her signature phasing scarf.

**Adjustments needed for final art:**
- Replace witch hat with the ethereal hooded scarf that phases between dimensions
- Hair should be brown in back, purple in front (not all purple)
- Eyes must glow golden (not standard), brighter when using power
- Add runes of Magick on the scarf that glow like star charts
- Outfit: low-cut halter top, low-rise jeans, tennis shoes, bracelets (not corset dress)

**Power color:** Soft purple + gold  
**School:** Divination

---

### Ref 3 — BLUE (Zeus / Soulstreamancer)
**Reference:** Brooding dark-haired man in long leather trench coat, fire/ember effects trailing from coat, hands in pockets, intimidating stance

**What it nails:** The brooding confidence, the leather jacket as signature, the raw elemental power trailing off the body, and the "bad-attitude heartthrob" silhouette are perfect. The fire/smoke trailing from the coat is a strong reference for how Blue's lightning should trail from his hands.

**Adjustments needed for final art:**
- Must be a Black man with brown/black messy hair
- Jacket should be open over bare chest (no shirt)
- Replace fire with blue-white lightning that sometimes becomes rainbow
- Add glowing blue eyes
- Swap trench coat for motorcycle-style leather jacket
- Blue jeans + motorcycle boots
- Portal effects = cosmic, dark, spiral-shaped

**Power color:** Blue-white lightning (sometimes rainbow)  
**School:** Universal / Soulstreamancy

---

### Ref 4 — CHANEL (Demon Queen / Vampyl Supreme Boss)
**Reference:** Tall dark-skinned woman with long blonde hair, black leather bodysuit with corset detail, crimson energy cape/wings, red-glowing orb, floating pose

**What it nails:** This is the strongest match in the set. The tall, commanding dark-skinned figure with blonde hair, the leather corseted villain aesthetic, the blood-red energy, and the luxury predator presence are almost exactly what the bible describes. The floating pose conveys her supernatural dominance.

**Adjustments needed for final art:**
- Emphasize the trans femme representation
- Exaggerated hourglass figure, long legs, 6 ft tall
- Add stiletto heels
- Power should read as "dark smoke + red glitch lines" not just red energy
- Blood Magick visual = red-black energy with a drain/siphon animation feel
- Seductive predator energy, not horror villain

**Power color:** Darkness with red highlights  
**School:** Blood Magick

### Ref 5 — MR. VIOLET (Shapeshifting Mascot / Gaia's Messenger)
**Reference:** Adorable squirrel creature with huge blue eyes, glowing purple galaxy-sparkle tail, small purple bow tie, innocent expression

**What it nails:** The purple glowing tail is the #1 non-negotiable element and it's perfectly captured here. The "dangerously cute" energy, the small companion scale, and the magical tail effect (galaxy sparkle) are excellent. This is exactly how a mascot familiar should read.

**Adjustments needed for final art:**
- Replace bow tie with purple yarn bracelet/bow on wrist (tied by Nina, charged with love Magick)
- The tail glow is perfect — keep the galaxy/cosmic quality
- Eyes can stay large and expressive
- Should feel like "the most powerful being in the room but chooses to be cute"
- Add subtle wisdom in the expression — he's ancient, not just adorable

**Power color:** Purple (specifically the tail glow)  
**Role:** Game mascot, legendary familiar

### Ref 6 — GUARDIAN (OZONE Assassin / God-Slayer)
**Reference:** Military-build man in dark tactical armor with cyan/teal glowing accents, futuristic rifle, confident combat stance, chest emblem

**What it nails:** The OZONE body suit concept, the tight-to-body functional armor with LED highlights, the futuristic rifle as signature prop, and the "serious, lethal" stance are all aligned with the bible. The chest emblem placement is where "OZONE" should go.

**Adjustments needed for final art:**
- Shift accent color from cyan to dark gunmetal + matrix green
- Add "OZONE" text visible on chest (not garish)
- Brown hair, green eyes, 5 o'clock shadow
- The suit should feel "bullet-proof and malleable, moves with the body"
- Gun should look "a century in the future" — even more advanced than the reference
- Athletic build, "world-class muscular"

**Power color:** Dark gunmetal + matrix green  
**School:** Abjuration / Conjuration

**Reference:** Blonde spiky-haired boy in worn t-shirt and cargo pants, fire sword, backpack/harness, youthful scrappy fighter stance

**What it nails:** The blonde hair, cargo pants with pockets, the young fighter energy, forearm gear, and the backpack/harness system are all close to Crux. The worn, street-level clothing captures his gypsy-punk aesthetic.

**Adjustments needed for final art:**

- Power effect = golden shimmer + orange-gold probability runes (not fire)
- Cargo pockets should be comically stuffed

**Power color:** Golden shimmer + orange-gold  
**School:** Fate

### Ref 9 — KIMMY (Baby Spiraar / Pink Werewolf)
**Reference:** Pink-haired cat-girl in sailor school uniform, fluffy pink tail, cat ears, cute demeanor, brown loafers with bows

**What it nails:** The all-pink color palette, school girl outfit, cute charm, and the "looks harmless but isn't" energy are perfect for Kimmy. The pleated skirt and sailor uniform match the bible.

## Motion & Sound Direction (Step 4)

### Heavy Hitters — Blue, Chanel, Guardian
Slow, weighty idle animations. Screen shake on impact. Blue = electrical crackle + arc hum. Chanel = pulsing heartbeat bass + dark smoke ambient. Guardian = mechanical weapon sounds + slight reverb.

### Speed Fighters — Yanoro, Surge-Overload
Snappy, twitchy idles. Yanoro = whoosh-to-silence (absence of sound is terrifying). Surge-Overload = digital glitch, keyboard clicks, holographic interface hum, data transfer SFX.

### Whimsical Fighters — Crux, Kimmy, Mr. Violet
Bouncy, energetic idles. Crux = boxer footwork, always grinning, coins jingling. Kimmy = "poof-to-growl" transformation audio. Mr. Violet = gentle tail flick + soft chime.

### Mystical — Kaery
Floaty, ethereal movement. Scarf phases in/out. SFX = wind chimes + cosmic resonance. Truth-sight = silence + golden harmonic pulse.

## Color Palette Summary (Step 5)

### Master Palette
- **Primary:** Deep violet/purple + aged gold + matte black + off-white
- **Foundation:** All characters rendered on pure black (game-ready sprite format)
- **Rule:** Contained glows, no bloom spam. Each character has ONE signature energy color.

## Style Gap Analysis (Step 8 — Explaining the Thinking)

### What the references establish well:
- **Rendering quality** is in the right range — semi-realistic, detailed, premium mobile
- **Black background sprite format** is consistent across all 9 — game-ready
- **Elemental glow effects** are contained and character-specific
- **Silhouettes are distinct** — you can tell each character apart in shadow
- **The dark-to-whimsical range** is represented (Chanel to Mr. Violet)

### What needs refinement for production:
- **Style consistency:** The references span from photorealistic render (Guardian, Blue) to softer illustration (Kimmy, Mr. Violet). Final art should converge toward the Joe Madureira / Battle Chasers middle ground — stronger line quality, bolder color blocking
- **Lore-specific details:** Signature props (Kaery's scarf, Crux's daggers, Guardian's OZONE chest text, Surge-Overload's holographic screen) are missing from references but are non-negotiable in final art
- **Racial/identity representation:** Blue must be Black, Kimmy must be Asian, Chanel must read as trans femme — the references establish the energy but not always the specific identity markers
- **Weapon/power accuracy:** Several references use fire/blade effects where the bible calls for different power types (lightning for Blue, golden shimmer for Crux, shadow smoke for Yanoro)
