# Soulstream Matchmancer — 5 Design Pillars

> Pin these where you can see them while working.
> Every feature, art, and scope decision flows through these.

---

## 1. Readable at a Glance

Every tile, icon, blocker, and UI element must read clearly at phone scale.
If it doesn't work at 60px, it doesn't ship.

- **Art:** Silhouette first, detail second
- **Code:** One system at a time, test immediately
- **Scope:** Cut anything that muddies the board

---

## 2. Dark Fantasy Means Restraint, Not Noise

Obsidian over chrome. Contained glow over sparkle spam.
Fewer, stronger ideas over cluttered compositions.
The premium feel comes from what you leave out.

- **Art:** Matte weathered surfaces, controlled cyan-violet energy
- **Code:** No feature unless it earns its complexity
- **Scope:** If it feels like casino energy, kill it

---

## 3. Every Mechanic Teaches the World

Blockers aren't just obstacles — they're Soulstream lore made playable.
If a mechanic can't connect to the fiction, question whether it belongs.
Players learn the world by playing, not reading.

- **Art:** Blockers and specials look like they belong to the lore
- **Code:** Mechanic introductions follow the narrative arc
- **Scope:** No orphan mechanics with no story reason to exist

---

## 4. One System, Proven, Then the Next

Never build two things at once.
Implement, test in Unity, commit, move on.

- **Art:** Tiles before characters before UI
- **Code:** Board > Visuals > Input > Matching > Specials > Blockers
- **Scope:** Sequence is survival for a solo dev

---

## 5. Ship the Ugly Truth, Then Polish

A working ugly game beats a beautiful broken one.
Placeholder squares that match correctly are worth more than gorgeous tiles in a crashing build.

- **Art:** Final assets drop in after mechanics work
- **Code:** Gameplay feel before visual juice
- **Scope:** All 25 levels playable before any level is pretty
