| Familiars | Familiar_[Name]_v[#].png | Familiar_MrViolet_v1.png |
| Chests | Chest_[Rarity]_v[#].png | Chest_Legendary_v1.png |
| Schools | School_[Name]_[Size].png | School_Necromancy_128.png |

## **Export Requirements**

Format: PNG with transparent background (black BG for generation, remove in post). Resolution: Maximum available, minimum 1024x1024 for game assets. Aspect Ratios: 1:1 for icons/dice/items, 2:3 for characters, 9:16 for full screens. Color Profile: sRGB for mobile display compatibility.
